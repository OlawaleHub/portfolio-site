(01) — Case studies

Web · Mobile · On-chain

01 Lagos Emergency A one-tap emergency reporting PWA for Lagos. Tap Police, Fire or Medical and it routes you to the nearest facility. Works with zero internet: the alert is queued in IndexedDB and replayed by a service worker when the connection returns. GPS on tap, with a 20-LGA dropdown fallback for when location is unavailable. → 02 Ifeoluwa Medical A publishing platform for a medical professional who writes for patients, not for other clinicians. An embedded Sanity Studio means the author publishes without a developer, while 60-second revalidation keeps the public site static and fast. Deliberately plain: evidence-based, no jargon, and a standing disclaimer that it is not medical advice. → 03 Token-Gated Voting A governance platform on Ethereum where influence is gated behind an ERC-20 token, built on OpenZeppelin's Governor framework. Paid voting with automatic refunds on cancellation, a 4% quorum, timelock execution and emergency pause. Vote weight is snapshotted from a past block, which is what makes flash-loan vote buying pointless. → 04 Refcrypt Labs Website The public platform for the studio I work at in Lekki — a production client build I own and maintain. Marketing surface, service architecture, CMS and on-page SEO for a nine-service blockchain and software practice. Source is confidential under NDA, so this page documents the approach rather than the code. → 05 Client Portfolio Builds Three portfolio sites built for a COO and marketing strategist, shipped to live domains on Vercel. Fast, static, mobile-first, and designed to be edited by the owner rather than by me. → 06 Cross-platform Mobile with Flutter Ongoing mobile work in Dart and Flutter: one codebase, Android and iOS, clean architecture and deliberate state management. API integration, local persistence and release builds, maintained as part of my role at Refcrypt Labs. →
